REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL — MUNICIPAL LED STREET LIGHTING MODERNISATION

The municipal authority intends to procure 1,200 outdoor LED road and street lighting luminaires for public roads.

Minimum technical requirements:
- Rated input power approximately 90 W per luminaire.
- Minimum luminous efficacy of 90 lm/W.
- Enclosure protection IP66.
- Surge protection shall be provided.
- Supply voltage: 230 V AC, 50 Hz.
- Luminaires shall be suitable for outdoor environmental conditions.
- Bidder shall provide product technical datasheets, test reports and traceability documents.
- Installation shall include mounting, electrical termination, aiming and commissioning.
- Acceptance shall include sample inspection, electrical safety checks, photometric verification and site performance verification.
- Warranty shall be minimum 5 years.

Standards clause:
The procuring entity will verify the applicability and current edition of relevant Indian Standards before issuing the final tender. The tender must identify applicable product, safety, performance, ingress-protection and EMC requirements, including the current applicable 2026 revision of the IS 10322 family rather than relying on generic wording.
